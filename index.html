Renewable energy & AI research

Bringing machine learning to power and energy systems.

grid voltage · 50 Hz · live anomaly detection0 anomalies flagged

I'm an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) and a Research Associate in the Power and Energy portfolio at Inspira Advisory & Consulting. My research integrates machine learning and deep learning with power and energy systems, from explainable anomaly detection in solar PV modules and PV performance analysis in hot-humid climates to electricity demand forecasting and data-integrity auditing of power-grid records. This builds on earlier work in signal processing and AI for healthcare, where I developed predictive models from wearable and ECG signals.
